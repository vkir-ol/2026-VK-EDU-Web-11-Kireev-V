How to play football like Cristiano Ronaldo?

asked by Dr. Pepper · 2 hours ago · 3 answers

I've been training for a year, but my free kicks still fly way over the goal.

What drills should I do every day to improve my technique, speed and dribbling? How many hours a week does it take to see real progress? Any advice on stamina and diet is also welcome.

Tags: football training

3 Answers

8

Start with the basics: 20 minutes of ball juggling every day, then work on your weak foot. For free kicks, place the ball and practise hitting it with the inside of your foot, not the toe. Film yourself and compare with slow-motion videos.

Don't forget about the gym. Strong legs and core make your shots more powerful and help you avoid injuries. Two or three workouts a week are enough.
